Privacy Policy for Spotnook

Application: Spotnook — Location Discovery & Place Finder
Package Name: com.spotnook.app • Target Platform: Android (Google Play)
Effective Date: November 8, 2026 • Last Updated: November 11, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

1. Overview & Commitment

Spotnook ("we", "our", or "us") provides a location discovery and place finder application designed to help users explore nearby cafes, restaurants, hotels, gyms, stores, hospitals, ATMs, and attractions.

Your privacy is our core priority. We adhere strictly to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and Google Play User Data Policies:

  • No Account Required: Spotnook operates in full guest mode by default. You are never required to register, create a username, or submit personal credentials.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or device data to any third party.
  • Minimal Data Collection: We only process device data that is strictly essential for nearby search and app performance.

2. Location Information Policy

Foreground-Only Location Access: Spotnook requests foreground location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) strictly when you tap "My Location" or request nearby place discovery.

  • Purpose of Collection: Coordinates are used exclusively to calculate Haversine distance, display venues on the interactive map, and sort discovery results by proximity to your current location.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Your location coordinates are transmitted ephemerally to map and place search APIs (OpenStreetMap Overpass API, Nominatim, or Google Places API) solely to process your search query. We do NOT log, track, or permanently retain your coordinates on remote servers.
  • No Background Location: Spotnook does NOT request or declare ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION. Spotnook never monitors or tracks your location when the application is minimized or closed.
  • Optional Permission & Manual Selection: Location permission is 100% voluntary. If you deny or revoke location access, Spotnook remains completely functional via manual city/neighborhood search, address autocomplete, or interactive map pin positioning.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

To provide mapping, venue information, and ad monetization, Spotnook incorporates select third-party services. These partners may process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

A. Advertising (Google AdMob & UMP)

Spotnook uses the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements:

  • Data Processed: Google may collect and process device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, general approximate location, diagnostic telemetry, and ad interaction logs for ad serving, fraud prevention, and analytics.
  • User Consent Management: We integrate the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), and worldwide can review, grant, or revoke their advertising consent at any time directly in the app under Settings > Ad & Privacy Consent Settings.
  • Ad Experience: Interstitial ads are strictly frequency-capped at natural screen transitions and never interrupt interactive map panning or navigation.
  • Learn more: Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

B. Mapping & Discovery Providers

  • OpenStreetMap (OSM) & Overpass API: Community mapping data retrieved in compliance with the Open Database License (ODbL). All map layers credit © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Google Places API (New): When enabled, Google Places API provides venue ratings, reviews, and opening hours in compliance with Google Maps Platform policies. Search terms and coordinates are processed without prohibited permanent caching. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Local Storage & User Data Deletion Rights

Spotnook values user ownership of data:

  • Local Storage Only: Your bookmarked places, search query history, and recently viewed venues are stored strictly on your local device via encrypted local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • One-Tap Complete Deletion: In compliance with the Google Play Account & Data Deletion Policy, you have total control to delete all stored information. Navigating to Settings > Privacy & Data Control > "Clear All Local Data" immediately wipes all bookmarks, search history, and cache from your device.
  • Uninstalling the application also completely removes all locally stored app data.

5. Phone Dialer & External Intents

When you tap the call button on a venue detail screen, Spotnook uses the Android system Intent.ACTION_DIAL with the tel: protocol. Spotnook does NOT request or require android.permission.CALL_PHONE. The phone dialer is handed off safely to your default system dialer without Spotnook accessing your call logs or executing calls directly.

6. Children's Privacy

Spotnook is designed for a general audience and is not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or minimum age applicable in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or process personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt steps to purge such data.

7. Data Security Safeguards

We employ industry-standard security safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and external APIs (Nominatim, Overpass, Google Places) are strictly encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3).
  • Cleartext Traffic Blocked: Android cleartext HTTP traffic is disabled across the app via strict Network Security Configuration.

8. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Union, UK, California, and others), you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to know what data is collected and processed.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all locally held data at any moment using the in-app "Clear All Local Data" feature.
  • Right to Opt-Out / Revoke Consent: You can revoke location permissions at any time via Android Device Settings, and revoke advertising consent via in-app Ad & Privacy Consent Settings.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app functionality or regulatory obligations. Any revisions will be published on this active webpage with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
